# ce-descubrimiento

For pasted text, monologue, file/link or parts presented as conversation/transcript, activate [ce-banco-transcripciones](../ce-banco-transcripciones/SKILL.md) before extracting matters or capturing synthesis. Keep received items exact, unknown identity and pending parts; don't infer a meeting from length. Load only the chapters needed for the task, the catalog and pertinent passages; full reception is neither comprehension nor approved knowledge.

1. Start with the purpose expressed by the owner and what already exists. Read the vNext-1.0R guide, chapters P00/P01/P01B/P01C and the catalog: `guia/PRIMERA_EMPRESA_CON_CODEX.md`.
2. **Before recovering legacy documents, determine their editorial admissibility.** If the owner already rejected texts, documents or a previous vault, don't read them to extract wording, don't index them nor use them as authority. You may inventory technical metadata without opening content when authorized. Read `docs/RECONSTRUCCION_EDITORIAL_Y_FUENTES.md`; don't ask again if the rejection was already explicit.
3. Search **only admissible and authorized sources**: originals of facts, current contracts or records, original consented conversations, documents reviewed by the expert and their experiences. Separate primary source, earlier synthesis and expressly rejected text. Indicate what you read complete/partial and what you couldn't verify.
4. Classify gaps as **K1** expert criterion not stated, **K2** system or document source, **K3** nobody knows yet, **K4** not needed for the task. Leave a brief record per material need in the work plan or CONTROL: matter, K class, original already consulted or pending locator, and next action. If K2 is accessible, recover it before asking; otherwise note the path or the missing permission. K3 stays as hypothesis and K4 doesn't block. Don't demand K2 numbers from the owner's memory nor turn this record into a form for them.
5. Listen first to the whole natural explanation and keep the matters that appear mixed. Extract a real episode, decision signals, exceptions, alternatives and a contrast case from the account itself. Ask only about the criterion not already stated nor recoverable; don't reduce the business to the first need or a five-question form.
6. **Before drafting D/X, activate `ce-arquitectura-conocimiento` if several matters appear.** Distinguish autonomous notes, existing notes and originals that must remain as source; run `brain_knowledge_plan` (the v2 plugin tool wrapping the upstream `plan-conocimiento` subcommand) in read-only mode and present the map to the owner. Avoid monolithic drafts and proliferation of empty notes. Then propose the appropriate D/X (D01–D07 and X01–X07) and explain **why** one block would suffice or needs an autonomous file. Never create 14 obligatory documents.
7. **Build substantive knowledge, not another battery of reservations.** Develop how the work functions and which signals, decisions and exceptions can be transferred to someone else; also keep the business lines the owner projects without converting them into current operations. The owner is the K1 source of their goals and criteria, without requiring an invoice to describe their strategy. Don't elevate a technical implementation to income. See `docs/SINTESIS_EMPRESARIAL_CONSTRUCTIVA.md`. If they already authorized building their vault, choose and develop the justified pieces without stopping to ask them to pick a first file.
8. **Materialize the authorized work:** record the original source and, if the account must be kept while you develop it, a provisional capture with `brain_capture`. The capture goes to `90_Sistema/Descubrimiento/`, never equals the final business content. Continue in the same task towards D/X or autonomous units with developed text, keeping their state in review, sources and relations. Read `docs/CAPTURA_PROGRESIVA.md`.
9. After writing, check `brain_status` and `brain_validate_vault`; run a concrete task from the knowledge via `ce-consulta`. Report path, ID, sources and warnings. If nothing was written, report `sin_archivo_generado` instead of hinting at progress in Obsidian.
10. Record continuity and pending items without turning them into truth.
**Output:** (a) business notes developed with the owner's criteria, not topic lists; (b) an instruction or task someone can carry out from them; (c) sources, material pending decisions and states in a brief CONTROL; (d) paths/IDs, `brain_status`/`brain_validate_vault` and the next concrete improvement. Don't allow K1–K4, reservations or provisional captures to displace content. Never confuse a long conversation with a created file.
