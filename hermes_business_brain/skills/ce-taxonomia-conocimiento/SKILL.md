# ce-taxonomia-conocimiento

Read the [common contract](../ce-banco-transcripciones/references/contrato-comun.md) for states, permissions, context and availability.

- **Trigger:** uncovered matter, doubtful alias, category/fusion/division or requested criterion change.
- **Inputs:** current categories, justification passages, scope/identity of entities, historical equivalences and target version.
- **Outputs:** multi-axis proposal with stable IDs, definition/inclusion/exclusion/examples, candidate aliases, versioned diff and reclassification queue. A single conversation can justify a useful category.
- **Errors:** `ENTITY_COLLISION`, `AMBIGUOUS_ALIAS`, `TAXONOMY_CYCLE`, `BASE_VERSION_STALE`; a homonymy is not fixed by automatic fusion.
- **Gate:** homonym fixtures and brand/company/project remain separated; fusions preserve provenance; each affected entry gets a job and non-delimitable cases open a full sweep of the catalog snapshot.

## Execution

Classify along independent axes (matter, function, entity, time/state and use), grounded in original passages. The agent decides meaning; word overlap doesn't decide identity, materiality nor category. A future idea keeps its state; classifying doesn't make it current. Never fuse company, brand, project and client by name or proximity. Record an ambiguous alias without resolving it arbitrarily.

Version the diff and the fuse/divide/rename equivalences, with scope and evidence. Don't delete historical IDs. Before consolidating, check the base version and the corresponding decision. Change creates an event and work for each affected entry; when evidence can't delimit it, open a full sweep of the snapshot via [antecedentes](../ce-revisar-antecedentes/SKILL.md). Don't apply massive substitutions in notes.
