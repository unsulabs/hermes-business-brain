# ce-desarrollo-empresa

Before the cycle, new conversational material enters via [ce-banco-transcripciones](../ce-banco-transcripciones/SKILL.md). Preservation, full analysis and background precede synthesis: delegate extraction to its flow, don't absorb it nor substitute the original with a CAP. Load chapters/metadata per task, not all guides or business sources. A business question uses ce-consulta; a category change opens taxonomy and a background sweep. Don't repeat installation acceptance in every daily session.

Coordinate the installation, discovery, architecture, documentation, query, changes and continuity skills. Read the D01–D07/X01–X07 catalog and, if the task includes communication, the [ce-documentacion](../ce-documentacion/SKILL.md) and [ce-consulta](../ce-consulta/SKILL.md) skills. The goal is to leave the person and their agents with knowledge that enables **working and deciding**, not just describing the business nor delivering a first note.

1. **Resume or install.** If a vault exists, recover identity, originals, notes, relations and version; never run `brain_create_vault` on it. If it doesn't exist, use `ce-instalacion`. Don't copy pilot data nor rejected legacy into another business. Acknowledge the session's authorizations and avoid asking for them again.
2. **Listen and recover without trapping the account in a form.** Gather admissible and accessible originals of the business and what the owner expresses naturally. Read the pertinent originals in full, including later corrections. Extract all material matters: activities, mechanisms, who decides, signals, episodes, exceptions, transferable patterns and possible futures. For each claim keep source, date, scope and proposed destination. For the current task, briefly record the material gaps as K1/K2/K3/K4 and their action: criterion only the owner can specify, original to recover, research to do or dispensable datum. Recover K2 in systems/documents before asking; ask K1 only if a criterion that changes the work is missing. This control neither occupies the business body nor forces filling four categories.
3. **Distribute by function and maintenance.** Build a D01–D07 coverage map by tasks and sources, without seven obligatory notes; use `ce-arquitectura-conocimiento` to decide reuse, revise, create or keep in source. X01–X07 and entities are created by identity, authority, currency or own history. A synthesis explains and links; an autonomous note keeps its changing conditions. If building is already authorized, don't stop drafting to request approval of every map.
4. **Develop content and transferable capability.** Use [ce-documentacion](../ce-documentacion/SKILL.md) to write in the business's working voice: what happens, how, why, which signals determine the choice, who can decide, what changes under an exception and what another person or agent can execute. Cross-check original→note to find omissions and note→original to detect inferences; keep future lines with their state instead of deleting them. The figures of one episode don't become policy, and a technical system doesn't prove sale or payment. Sources, limits and editing go in CONTROL without replacing the body. Materialize the supported pieces in review with IDs, links and backup where appropriate. A provisional CAP does not fulfill this deliverable.
5. **Use and correct.** Execute a concrete task via `ce-consulta`: prepare a decision, an instruction, a conversation, an offer or a piece of communication according to the real need. Test an exception and a datum that must stay uncertain. Another session or person must be able to recover the source and reproduce the criterion without the originating conversation. If the output is generic or incomplete, trace the failure to source, extraction, document, relation or retrieval and repair there. Record the raw output and the owner's correction; don't declare success by CI, note count or literal search.
6. **Maintain the brain daily.** A new conversation, client, file or decision triggers an impact review with `ce-continuidad` and [ce-cambios](../ce-cambios/SKILL.md): what updates, what keeps history, what new identity appears and what instruction for people/agents changes. Re-read originals, preserve IDs, states, permissions and versions. Don't schedule tasks nor publish content without an express decision.
7. **Close only the task actually proven.** Run `brain_status`, `brain_validate_vault` and the Obsidian/Bases check-up where appropriate. Report what work each piece made possible, its source, state and test; identify only the material source or decision missing for the next use. A complete business requires several representative tasks and owner review, not a first piece.

**Output:** substantive and connected business documents in the authorized vault; a task performed from them with evidence and corrections; continuity record and coverage by function. Apply the acceptance criteria of the bundled [ce-calidad-repositorio](../ce-calidad-repositorio/SKILL.md) skill to the authorized instance; the synthetic examples verify isolation and rollback.
