# Area templates - first session

Eight guided-open-question templates, one per business area, used by the [ce-primera-sesion](../../SKILL.md) skill. Run them one by one, starting wherever the owner has most to say; each template is a guide, never a form to be filled in.

| # | Area | Template | One-line summary |
|---|------|----------|------------------|
| 1 | Business model | [area-01-business-model.md](area-01-business-model.md) | How the business really creates, delivers and captures value today, and which assumptions remain untested. |
| 2 | Market, customers and brand | [area-02-market-customers.md](area-02-market-customers.md) | Who buys, why, through which episodes - and the brand signals the market actually sees. |
| 3 | Offer and commercial system | [area-03-offer-commercial.md](area-03-offer-commercial.md) | What can be contracted today, its scope and exclusions, and the real path from opportunity to agreement. |
| 4 | Execution and processes | [area-04-execution-processes.md](area-04-execution-processes.md) | How the work actually happens: observed flow, decision points, exceptions and quality gates. |
| 5 | Economy and finance | [area-05-economy-finance.md](area-05-economy-finance.md) | How money moves, which figures the owner trusts, and the criteria for money decisions. |
| 6 | People and roles | [area-06-people-roles.md](area-06-people-roles.md) | Who does what, who can decide what, and the tacit knowledge that only one person holds. |
| 7 | Risks and commitments | [area-07-risks-commitments.md](area-07-risks-commitments.md) | What would really hurt if it failed, and which commitments are proven by documents. |
| 8 | Direction and decisions | [area-08-direction-decisions.md](area-08-direction-decisions.md) | Strategic boundaries, priorities, and the decision record with authority and dates. |

## How the skill uses them

For each area the agent: (1) explains the area as a conversation guide; (2) runs the template's open questions and listens for episodes, criteria, exceptions and contrast cases; (3) captures everything presented with brain_capture (identity, date, scope) as provisional custody; (4) records gaps as K1/K2/K3/K4; and (5) after brain_knowledge_plan, opens only the justified D/X documents or autonomous notes from the mapping table. The External-AI prompt of each template can be pasted into any external assistant; the pasted transcript re-enters as a source through brain_capture.

Registered as `hermes-business-brain:ce-primera-sesion`; the templates are its `references/area-templates/`.
