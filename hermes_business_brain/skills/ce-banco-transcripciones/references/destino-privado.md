# Authorized local integration into a private vault

Shared contract included in the autonomous skills distribution.

The default destination keeps the restriction to `interno`. For an authorized private vault, `render_source` receives absolute `vault` and `destination_profile = {"path": "/path/to/private/policy.json", "sha256": "bytes hash"}`. `prepare_batch` receives the same binding in its snapshot. Candidates and manifest keep it; every access validation in apply re-reads the policy. Changing it requires rendering and preparing again.

The policy JSON contains `schema: 1`, `active: true`, absolute `vault` without symlinks, `company`, `audience`, `purposes: ["local_analysis", "internal_query"]`, `allowed_sensitivities`, `source_sensitivity` (explicit TR → interno/confidencial/restringido map) and `authorization: {"responsable": "…", "referencia": "evidence of the owner's authorization"}`. A missing TR is rejected. There is no global exception by private boolean. The hash binds declared bytes; it doesn't authenticate the person nor implement OS ACL. The operator resolves Obsidian (brain_resolve_vault) and checks authority and privacy before creating the external policy.

The policy doesn't grant source permissions: current local_analysis and internal_query are checked per version/business/audience, plus hashes of original and analysis. It doesn't serve to export, train or publish. Effective sensitivity keeps the maximum between declared classification, policy, dependencies and previous note. Cards, full views, map and new Bases carry sensitivity. Existing Bases are generic queries and aren't rewritten; Inicio/Como_usar that already link the corpus don't change either. New navigation in Inicio/Como_usar keeps body and other properties and raises sensitivity when appropriate. An existing full view with lower classification is rejected for explicit review, not rewritten.

For a delivery with new IDs, create a new external policy version with those explicit TRs and their authorization evidence; keep intact the bytes of previous sealed policies. Also record source permissions for the new versions. A persistent authorization covering future deliveries avoids repeating the question, but doesn't automatically add the IDs to the historical profile nor to the permission chain. The [default flow](flujo-predeterminado.md) requires persisting this configuration privately, without including it in the distributable product.

Originals and bank versions remain immutable. The same manifest-bound authorization controls, SHA, external backup, fencing and journal keep applying. Rollback keeps its exact-restoration function and rejects later edits. Withdrawal of new cards can be completed after revoking the policy; historical transitions keep their previous access controls. Reading restored material remains subject to source permissions.

Synthetic tests: `hermes plugins doctor hermes-business-brain --ci` (or the plugin repo tests via its `.venv`: `python -m pytest tests/ -q`). They cover the three levels, wrong destination, changed policy, missing TR, rejected export, revocation, sensitivity downgrade, navigation and restore/post-edit. They don't attest GUI nor business semantic acceptance.
