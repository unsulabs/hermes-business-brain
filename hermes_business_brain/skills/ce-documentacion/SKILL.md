# ce-documentacion

A transcript is preserved and analyzed via [ce-analizar-transcripcion](../ce-analizar-transcripcion/SKILL.md) before its note. The logical note links the complete original, version/hash, all parts and destinations; it does not replace the original with a summary nor constitute automatic integration. Verify original citations in this session, with UTF-8/versioned spans and context. Keep uncertainty and proposals. Sensitivity is not lowered to publish: confidential/restricted sources stay private while the destination does not admit their permissions. Do not assume the D/X applier writes notes or entities; check the interface. Load only the specialized catalog/chapter and pertinent sources, walking the needed complete originals in batches.

**0. Mandatory gate before drafting D/X or a long note:** activate `ce-arquitectura-conocimiento`. Inventory matters, existing notes and the canonical owner of each idea. If strategy, offer, operation, decision, episode or research lives in a narrative, first show a reuse/update/create/do-not-create map and run the product's knowledge-planning flow read-only with `brain_knowledge_plan` (the v2 plugin tool wrapping the upstream `plan-conocimiento` subcommand). The D/X synthesize and link; they do not absorb all independently-living pieces. The user does not have to ask for the decomposition.

1. Consult the canonical classes in `hermes_business_brain/data/modelo/clases_documentales.json` and the per-class template under `90_Sistema/Plantillas/` in the vault template. **The template `plantilla_boveda/90_Sistema/Plantillas/DXX_...md` is only a skeleton**; it never authorizes publishing empty text.
2. Read fully each pertinent original and its later corrections. Before drafting, break down its material claims (activity, mechanism, criterion, episode, alternative, exception, future line) with a locator and decide for each one a canonical destination or conservation in source with a reason. Cross-check **original → note** to detect omitted ideas and **note → original** to detect invented claims. The coverage map is an internal control, not a substitute for drafting. Apply P01 (admissible sources) → P01B (K1/K2/K3/K4) → P01C (episodes and elicitation). If the user rejected a draft, do not reuse it; follow the editorial reconstruction rules of the bundled `ce-revisar-antecedentes` and `ce-documentacion` protocols.
3. Gates: G1 sources read; G2 expert judgment; G3 episode; G4 limits/exceptions; G5 correct authority; G6 business particularity; G7 utility in a task.
4. **Develop business content before a deficiencies report.** Re-read the "business document vs. CONTROL" distinction and the constructive-synthesis rules of this protocol. The business narrative must explain the work being done, mechanisms, decisions, observed capabilities, relevant operational limits and why they matter; without suggesting sales, successes or leadership without source. Use the **structure appropriate to its class**. Metadata, partial reads, sources, permissions, conflicts, uncertainties and blocking reasons go mainly in CONTROL, unless they materially alter a reader's decision. Avoid generic jargon, invented slogans and "AI copy". The owner's statement can support purpose, strategy and professional judgment; it does not replace contracts or measurements.
5. Prepare a **working version readable even when G1–G7 do not allow final approval**: it contains what is actually known, separates hypotheses and offers the next useful analysis; the gates block its promotion to canonical, not the reasoning or the proposals. Evaluate the coverage map against all pertinent originals and P03 A–K: a material idea of the owner does not disappear because it sounds uncomfortable or future; it stays developed with its state or explicitly retained in source. Re-read the note as a team member who will use it: can they decide or execute something with their criteria, without turning the company into an externally-observed case? If the answer is no, redo content, do not add disclaimers. No offer, payment, commercial result or market preference is deduced from rhetoric.
6. If the owner already authorized building or correcting the vault, develop and register the piece as under review with source, backing and diff as appropriate; do not ask permission again for every note. Architecture and meaning are contrasted against their original statements and corrections. Ask for their decision when there is an ambiguous business alternative, an editorial approval or an external publication. Use the vNext-0.9 handover contract if a valid manifest exists; otherwise register the note as under review with `brain_document` (the v2 plugin tool for the upstream `documento` subcommand): `brain_document --vault <vault> --clase ... --titulo ... --archivo ... --estado en_revision` — with sources and precise pending items. In each D/X, assign stable comments `<!-- kb:id=D01-B001 -->` or `<!-- kb:id=X02-ID-B001 -->` to the substantive fragments that must be retrieved, corrected or versioned separately; the prefix must match the real `doc_id`. Keep those IDs in later revisions and check with `brain_validate_vault` that the expected blocks appear. Do not add a block per paragraph nor invent content to raise the count. If a document ends with no blocks, record the reason and do not declare contract criterion E2 satisfied. A registered note is not approved by the act of writing it.
7. When changing a fact, prepare a proposal with `ce-cambios` before overwriting previously approved information. If a material need stays open, keep in CONTROL its class K1/K2/K3/K4, the locator of what was consulted and the next action; do not hide it behind a generic "missing data" nor fill a category that does not apply.
**Output:** (1) substantive, specific, usable business text, as working draft or revised document as appropriate; (2) separate CONTROL with evidence, sources, states, conflicts and gates G1–G7; (3) a decision or next research step that will actually improve the piece. If it only contains pending items, the output fails and must be developed, not presented as a company description.
