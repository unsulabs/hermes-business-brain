# ce-investigacion

Retrieve first the admissible internal research per task, including what is preserved in the corpus via [ce-revisar-antecedentes](../ce-revisar-antecedentes/SKILL.md). Separate external data from internal identity/voice, owner hypotheses from client facts. A website or summary reproducing rejected content does not rehabilitate it. Cite sources actually opened, with version/date and scope; do not load all of the company's sources for a narrow external question.

1. Formulate the concrete business question, population, context, date and the decision it must serve.
2. Read pertinent internal documents and public/primary sources when authorized; contrast original evidence against previous research.
3. Capture method, sample, date, scope, biases, counter-evidence and results with identifiers. Do not assume the owner's opinions are the market's voice.
4. Separate observations, derived calculations, hypotheses, proposed experiments and applicable conclusions. In legal/tax topics of the applicable jurisdiction, identify the competent authority, the normative version and professional review.
5. If the research creates new independent knowledge, justify the `investigacion` entity or X07; otherwise integrate a referenced block.
6. Validate which offers, messages or processes would be affected before proposing changes.
**Output:** evidence and sources + bounded findings + open questions + a concrete enabled decision.
