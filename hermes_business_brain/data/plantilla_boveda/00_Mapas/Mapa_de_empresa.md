# Business needs map of {{EMPRESA_NOMBRE}}

This map lists **business questions**; it does not force 12 departments, nor does it create fictitious notes. Ask the agent to develop the priority need with the bundled skills.

| Perspective | Question for the agent | Usual sources |
|---|---|---|
| Direction and strategy | Why does the business exist, where does it create value, what does it choose not to do? | Owner/decision maker and dated decisions |
| Market and brand | What do we know about real customers versus hypotheses? | Interviews, sales, studies, and episodes |
| Offer | What is offered, price, scope, and current exclusions? | Authorized catalog, particular agreements |
| Commercial | What was quoted, accepted, contracted, delivered, and collected? | Proposals, contracts, CRM, accounting |
| Operations | How is delivery done, what exceptions and quality evidence exist? | Executor, cases, observed processes |
| Projects | What is active, in preparation, blocked, or closed? | Files, minutes, deliverables, and owners |
| Economy | Where are costs, cash, commitments, and scenarios confirmed? | Accounting, bank, budgets |
| Organization | Who decides, works, delegates, and substitutes? | Agreements and authorized people |
| Resources and dependencies | Which tools, materials, suppliers, and systems are critical? | Inventory system, contracts, operations |
| Legal, privacy, and risks | Which proven obligations or controls apply? | Legal sources and competent counsel |
| Learning | Which experiences justify changing our criteria? | Cases, evaluations, and decisions |
| Technology and AI | Which tool can read/write where and under what permission? | Systems and access policies |

The states `desconocido`, `hipotesis`, `pendiente` and `conflicto` are valid outcomes when the sources do not allow saying more.
