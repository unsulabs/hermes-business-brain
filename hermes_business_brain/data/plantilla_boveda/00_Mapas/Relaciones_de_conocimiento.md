# Knowledge relationships

Typed relationships are recorded by ID, source, direction, domain, and state under 90_Sistema/Relaciones. The fact that one note links to another does NOT approve the link.

To query the note network, the agent runs a **read-only knowledge map**. After review and explicit authorization it may publish the derived map with exact confirmation and an external backup. Then 00_Mapas/Mapa_Derivado_Conocimiento.md and its associated Canvas with links and arrows will appear.

Both are **derived indexes**, not official sources; their content may become stale until regenerated. If no notes or links have been registered yet, that is normal and does not require inventing records.
